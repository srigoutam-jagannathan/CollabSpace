**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Branch:** `refact/UI/coffee-palette-makeover` @ `d0ae9ec` (with uncommitted working tree)
**Scope:** 32 routes, 27 source modules, 10,645 LOC

**Formatted report:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/34bac52e-0490-4ab4-8531-9956face9cbb

---

## Method and its limits

Every finding below was traced in code and, where checkable, verified by running
something: contrast ratios computed from the real theme tokens, `Btn` call sites
counted by a brace-balancing parser, layout behaviour reasoned from the
primitive's own JSX.

This was **not** a visual pass. The iOS platform component is not installed on
the review machine, and the auth gate plus five unapplied migrations would stall
a simulator run at the sign-in screen. Findings tagged **verify visually** are
the ones where seeing the pixels could change the conclusion.

# P0 — Blocking

## 01. The privacy control does nothing

Settings offers Public / University / Private profile visibility. The value is
written to `AsyncStorage` under `privacy_prefs` and never read again — not by
the talent query, not by search, not by any RLS policy. `fetchTalents()` selects
every profile row unfiltered, and because the preference never leaves the device,
no server-side rule could honour it even in principle.

A student who sets their profile to Private stays fully listed and browsable by
every other user. On a platform where someone may choose that setting for
personal-safety reasons, this is the most serious issue in the app.

- `app/(app)/settings/index.tsx:269`
- `src/features/projects/DiscoveryFeed.tsx:369`

**Fix:** move visibility to a `profiles` column and enforce it in an RLS policy.
Until that ships, remove the control rather than leave a setting that misstates
what the system does.

## 02. Group chat silently discards failed messages

`HandshakeChat.sendMessage` clears the input, then awaits the insert without ever
destructuring `error`. A failed send leaves no message, no alert, and no typed
text to recover — the sender believes it went through and nobody receives it.
Project group chat is the core collaboration surface.

`DirectChat.sendMessage` handles exactly this case correctly; the group path
simply never got the same treatment.

- `src/features/chat/HandshakeChat.tsx:269` (broken)
- `src/features/chat/DirectChat.tsx:126` (correct reference implementation)

**Fix:** mirror DirectChat's handling, then move both to optimistic send with a
per-bubble failed state and tap-to-retry.

## 03. No accessibility affordances anywhere

Zero occurrences across the codebase of `accessibilityLabel`, `accessibilityRole`,
`accessibilityHint`, `accessibilityState`, `accessibilityValue`, `allowFontScaling`.
The tab bar is icon-only with no labels, so VoiceOver announces five unlabelled
buttons; avatars, the notification bell, every icon button and every chip are
equally opaque.

Dynamic Type is also ignored. `fs()` carries the docstring *"respects the device's
accessibility text-size setting"*, but calls `PixelRatio.roundToNearestPixel`
(screen-width scaling) and never `PixelRatio.getFontScale()`.

- `src/utils/scale.ts:44`
- `app/(app)/_layout.tsx:22`

**Fix:** add role and label defaults inside `Btn` so one change covers most of the
app; wire `getFontScale()` into `fs()`; label the tab bar. QUB and Ulster are
public bodies — an app aimed at their students would be expected to meet WCAG 2.2 AA.

## 04. The `Btn` primitive silently drops row layout

`Btn` puts the caller's `style` on the `Pressable`, then wraps all children in a
single `Animated.View` for the press-scale transform. Any `flexDirection: "row"`,
`gap` or `justifyContent` passed to `Btn` applies to that wrapper — never to the
children, which stack in a default column.

Six call sites hit this, including every checkbox row in the search filter panel
(checkbox renders above its label) and the filter count badge (below the icon
rather than beside it).

- `src/components/Btn.tsx:60` (root cause)
- `app/(app)/search.tsx:209, 280, 327`
- `app/(app)/users/[id].tsx:84, 133`
- `app/(app)/projects/[id]/join.tsx:89`

**Fix:** move `style` onto the `Animated.View` and leave the `Pressable` carrying
only the pressed opacity. **Verify visually** afterwards — some sites may have
been laid out around the broken behaviour.

## 05. There is no password recovery

"Forgot password?" is rendered, styled in the accent colour and fully pressable —
and its handler is `onPress={() => {}}`. A user who forgets their password has no
route back into the account anywhere in the app.

- `app/(auth)/onboarding.tsx:276`

**Fix:** wire `supabase.auth.resetPasswordForEmail` with the `collabspace://`
redirect. If that can't ship now, hide the control.

## 06. Delete Account walks through two confirmations, then fails

The flow presents a destructive confirm, then a second "Final Confirmation", then
invokes the `delete-account` function — and its `catch` reports *"Account deletion
is not yet available. Please contact support@collabspace.app."* regardless of what
actually failed.

The Edge Function is written and correct; it is simply not deployed. So the message
is wrong in both directions: it denies a feature that exists, and once deployed it
will still report a transient network error as a missing feature. Right-to-erasure
makes this more than a UX concern.

- `app/(app)/settings/index.tsx:256`
- `supabase/functions/delete-account/index.ts`

**Fix:** deploy the function, then report the real error. Collapse the two
confirmations into one requiring a typed confirmation word.

## 07. "Remember me" is decorative

The checkbox has state, a spring animation and haptics. `rememberMe` is read at
exactly three places — all styling the checkbox itself. Supabase persists the
session either way, so a user who deliberately unchecks it on a shared machine
stays signed in.

- `app/(auth)/onboarding.tsx:194`

**Fix:** make it select a non-persisting session store, or remove it.

# P1 — Significant friction

## 08. No pull-to-refresh on any screen

Not one `RefreshControl` or `onRefresh` across 21 list and scroll screens. The only
way to refresh Discover, chat, notifications or projects is to navigate away and back.

**Fix:** add to the four primary lists first.

## 09. Returning to a tab wipes the feed to a spinner

`useFocusEffect` calls `fetchProjects()`, which sets `loadingProjects = true`, and
the loading branch replaces the entire list with a centred `ActivityIndicator`.
Opening a project and pressing back flashes a full-screen loader over content the
app already has.

- `src/features/projects/DiscoveryFeed.tsx:485, 539`

**Fix:** keep stale content on screen; spinner only when there is nothing to show.

## 10. Every list fetches the entire table

`fetchProjects` and `fetchTalents` issue no `.limit()` or `.range()`. Talents is
`select(*) from profiles order by pvs_score` — every student, on every visit.

**Fix:** range-based pagination with `onEndReached`.

## 11. Failed loads are presented as emptiness

Six queries destructure only `data` and drop `error`. When one fails the state
becomes `[]` and the user sees "No other students yet." — the app reports an empty
platform instead of a failure, with no retry.

## 12. Messages only appear after the server round-trip

Neither chat renders optimistically; a sent message appears when the realtime echo
arrives. On campus wifi that gap is visible.

## 13. No unsaved-changes protection on any form

No `beforeRemove` listener or equivalent anywhere. Swiping back out of Create
Project, Edit Profile or Portfolio Links discards everything typed, without warning.

## 14. Password managers cannot fill anything

`PremiumInput` accepts an `autoComplete` prop and no call site passes it;
`textContentType` appears nowhere. No keychain autofill on any auth screen.

**Fix:** pass `autoComplete` / `textContentType` of `username`, `password`,
`new-password` on the three auth forms.

## 15. Sign-up never mentions email confirmation

On success the flow advances straight to skill selection. If email confirmation is
enabled on the Supabase project (the default), no session exists, so after choosing
skills the user is bounced to sign-in with no explanation — and the skills are
written under the `!user` branch that skips the database entirely.

**Fix:** **verify** the project's confirmation setting first. If on, add a "check
your inbox" state and hold skill selection until the session exists.

## 16. Feedback is delivered by 33 blocking alerts

Eleven titled just "Error". System alerts interrupt, must be dismissed, and cannot
carry a retry that keeps context — including for routine load failures. One,
"Project created but no ID returned.", exposes internal state in developer language.

**Fix:** reserve `Alert` for destructive confirmations; inline errors for forms,
a lightweight toast for transient outcomes.

## 17. Buttons that look disabled but aren't

Sign-up and skill selection animate the CTA to `opacity: 0.45` when incomplete but
never pass `disabled` — fully pressable while looking inert. The dimming wrapper
also encloses the "Have an account? Sign in" link, greying out a valid navigation
action.

- `app/(auth)/onboarding.tsx:351, 455`

## 18. Chat has no date separators and no history pagination

Every bubble shows a bare `toLocaleTimeString`. A message from three weeks ago is
indistinguishable from one sent this morning. All messages load at once, and the
list is not `inverted`, so first paint scrolls after layout.

## 19. The tab bar gives no orientation

Five icon-only tabs, no labels. Active state is colour alone — and inconsistent:
Home and Search highlight in cream `C.white` while Chat and Profile highlight in
caramel `C.pink`, in the same bar. Four routes are registered `href: null`, so
while in Projects, Notifications, Settings or another user's profile, no tab is
focused at all.

- `app/(app)/_layout.tsx:25-43, 265-268`

## 20. Discover's error state belongs to the wrong tab

`error` is only ever set by `fetchProjects`, but the error branch renders above
both tabs and its Retry always calls `fetchProjects`. Fail on Projects, switch to
Talents, and you get the projects error with a retry that refetches projects.

## 21. Connect and Invite give no feedback while working — or when they fail

`onConnect` writes state only on success (`if (!error) setConnectionStatuses(...)`).
A failed insert produces nothing — no spinner during, no error after. Separately, a
declined request renders as "Not Available", which reads as a statement about the
other person rather than about the request.

## 22. Talents never refresh after first load

`handleTabChange` fetches talents only `if (talents.length === 0)`, and the focus
effect refreshes statuses but not the list. New students never appear until restart.

# P2 — Consistency and polish

## 23. Contrast failures in both themes

Computed from the real tokens against the real grounds, WCAG 2.2 relative
luminance, alpha composited.

| Token | Against | Ratio | AA |
|---|---|---|---|
| textFaint — dark | `#1a0e0c` | 2.24 | **Fail** |
| textFaint — light | `#faf0e4` | 1.82 | **Fail** |
| textMuted — light | `#faf0e4` | 3.09 | Large only |
| purpleL — light | `#faf0e4` | 4.10 | Large only |
| yellow — light | `#faf0e4` | 2.78 | **Fail** |
| CTA label — gradient start | `#c08552` | 2.97 | **Fail** |
| CTA label — gradient mid | `#a06840` | 4.38 | Marginal |
| textMuted — dark | `#1a0e0c` | 5.11 | Pass |

The CTA line matters most: the primary button gradient runs `#c08552 → #8c5a3c` in
**both** themes, and its centred cream label sits over the failing half. Every
"Sign In", "Create Account" and "Continue" is affected.

**Fix:** darken the gradient's start stop, or set the CTA label to espresso
`#4b2e2b`. Darken `textMuted` and `textFaint` in the light theme.

## 24. 180 hardcoded colour literals bypass the token system

Concentrated in notifications (30), HandshakeChat (23), ProjectInsights (20),
settings (19). Most are coffee-palette rgba values — they look right today but
cannot flip with the theme. Seven are cream-tinted (`rgba(255,248,240,…)`), a
dark-mode assumption that renders near-invisible in light mode, including every
`PremiumInput` background and every skill chip.

Three genuine old-palette leftovers survive:

- `src/features/projects/DiscoveryFeed.tsx:515` — bell badge ring `rgba(18,0,45,1)`
- `app/(app)/projects/index.tsx:90` — spinner still `#3b82f6` blue
- `src/components/Avatar.tsx:56` — unread dot border hardcoded `#120908`
- `src/components/PremiumInput.tsx:143` — input background ignores `C.inputBg`

## 25. The type scale has five names and three weights

`FONT.regular` and `FONT.medium` both resolve to `Lato_400Regular`; `FONT.semiBold`
and `FONT.bold` both resolve to `Lato_700Bold`. Call sites reaching for
`FONT.medium` to lift something above body weight render identically to the text
around them.

**Fix:** load Lato 500/600, or collapse the tokens to the three that exist.

## 26. The Belfast Lock rejection screen was never migrated

`app/(auth)/access-restricted.tsx` is one of two files still on NativeWind classes,
and its classes are the **old** slate/blue system — `bg-app`, `text-slate-400`,
`bg-surface-card`. A user rejected by domain validation lands on the one screen
still wearing the previous design. It also uses a raw `Pressable`, so its button
has none of the press feedback every other button has.

## 27. Two skill pickers, duplicated verbatim

`SKILL_CATEGORIES` and `SkillChip` are copied identically into
`app/(auth)/onboarding.tsx` and `app/(onboarding)/skills.tsx`. The same user meets
both — once at sign-up, once from Settings — in two different layouts. Editing one
list silently drifts from the other.

## 28. Three comments describe behaviour the code doesn't have

- Onboarding parallax is commented "native-driven, zero JS overhead" while its
  `Animated.event` passes `useNativeDriver: false` — every scroll frame crosses the
  bridge, on the longest scroll surface in the app.
- `fs()` claims Dynamic Type support it does not implement (finding 03).
- `GradientBtn` documents a "purple→pink gradient"; `GradientBg` labels its
  ellipses "pink radial".

**Fix:** switch the scroll driver to native and correct the comments — a wrong
comment about performance is how a real regression stays hidden.

## 29. Motion has no reduced-motion path and one animation never stops

`AccessibilityInfo.isReduceMotionEnabled` is never consulted. The onboarding
"breathing halo" runs an `Animated.loop` with no stop condition for as long as the
screen is mounted. `useEntrance` staggers 80ms per element over a 560ms fade —
eight elements take ~1.2s before the last lands.

## 30. Cold start shows a blank gradient

Nothing calls `SplashScreen.preventAutoHideAsync`; the native splash hides as soon
as the root view mounts, before `useFonts` resolves. The root renders
`{fontsLoaded && <Slot />}`, and the app layout's loading branch returns a bare
`<View style={{flex:1}} />` — the first moments are an empty gradient, no logo, no
spinner.

Related: `(Text as any).defaultProps` is used at module scope to apply Lato
globally. `defaultProps` on function components was removed in React 19, which
Expo SDK 54 ships. **Verify visually** that the default font still applies.

## 31. Press feedback is inconsistent

`Btn` exists so every interactive element has scale-and-opacity feedback, and it is
imported in 18 files — but raw `Pressable` is still used for the Discover tab
switcher, the Create Project back button, the "Remember me" row and the terms
checkbox. Tab-bar presses also skip the haptic that chips and toggles fire.
Back navigation uses `ChevronLeft` in 25 places and `ArrowLeft` in 2.

## 32. The skills screen's bottom bar breaks the background

The fixed CTA bar paints `backgroundColor: C.bgDeep` (`#120908`) while the page
behind it is the `GradientBg` radial over `#1a0e0c`. The two don't match, so the
bar reads as an opaque rectangle pasted over the gradient. **Verify visually.**

## 33. Hiding your university badge only hides it from you

`showUniversityBadge` lives in device-local `privacy_prefs` and is read only by
`app/(app)/profile.tsx` — the screen showing you your *own* profile. Other users
viewing you through `users/[id].tsx` can't read your device's storage, so the badge
stays visible to everyone else. The toggle appears to work because the only person
who can see its effect is the person who set it.

# Suggested sequencing

Ordered by trust recovered per hour spent, not by severity alone.

1. **Stop the bleeding — half a day.** Findings 02, 05, 07, 33. Add the missing
   error check in group chat, then remove or wire the three controls that promise
   something they don't deliver. Deleting a dead control takes minutes and
   immediately makes the app honest.
2. **Fix the primitive — one hour.** Finding 04. One change inside `Btn` repairs
   six screens, including the search filter panel. Cheapest visible win here.
3. **Privacy, properly — one day.** Finding 01. Visibility moves to a column with
   an RLS policy behind it. Do this before any external testing, not after.
4. **Perceived speed — one to two days.** Findings 08, 09, 11, 12, 21.
   Pull-to-refresh, stale-while-revalidate lists, optimistic send, real failure
   states. Most changes how finished the app feels.
5. **Accessibility pass — two days.** Findings 03 and 23. Defaults in `Btn`, labels
   on the tab bar and icon buttons, `getFontScale()` in `fs()`, contrast
   corrections. Largely mechanical once the primitives are done.
6. **Consistency sweep — ongoing.** Findings 24-32. Migrate `access-restricted`,
   de-duplicate the skill picker, pull cream-tinted literals into tokens, fix the
   three misleading comments.
